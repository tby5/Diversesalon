Hair Extensions

Instant length, volume, and color in no time. Our extensive knowledge of hair extensions will guide you towards choosing the best option for your hair type. We carry premium brands and virgin unprocessed hair right in our retail area.

Why Choose Our Extensions

At Diverse Salon, we specialize in finding the perfect extension match for every client. Our stylists have extensive knowledge of different textures, lengths, and installation methods to help you achieve your desired look.

We carry a wide range of premium hair options from trusted brands, including virgin unprocessed hair. Whether you're looking for a subtle enhancement or a dramatic transformation, we have the expertise and inventory to make it happen.

All of our extension services are designed to keep your natural hair healthy while giving you the length, volume, and color you've always wanted.

The Benefits of Hair Extensions

⏱️

Instant Transformation

Get the length, volume, and fullness you want without waiting for your natural hair to grow. See results immediately.

🎨

Experiment with Color

Try bold colors and styles without committing to permanent changes. Perfect for testing new looks before going all-in.

🛡️

Protective Styling

Extensions provide protective styles that help minimize damage to your natural hair while you achieve your desired look.

💪

Added Volume

If you have fine or thin hair, extensions add fullness and thickness for a more voluminous style that lasts.

✨

Versatility

With extensions, you can style your hair in countless ways. Create updos, braids, waves, and more with confidence.

♻️

Reusable Options

Many of our extension methods are reusable and can be removed and reinstalled, making them a smart investment.
